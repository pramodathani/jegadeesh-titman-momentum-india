# indian_transaction_costs.py

Statutory charges for an NSE delivery (CNC) trade as of 2026, as fractions of value traded:

| Charge | Rate | Side |
|---|---|---|
| Securities transaction tax | 0.1% | buy and sell |
| Stamp duty | 0.015% | buy |
| NSE transaction charge | 0.00297% | buy and sell |
| SEBI turnover fee | Rs 10 per crore, 0.0001% | buy and sell |
| GST | 18% of exchange, SEBI and brokerage charges | buy and sell |
| Brokerage | 0 at discount brokers for delivery | — |

Depository charges of about Rs 15 per scrip per sell day are left out, because they depend on order size rather than value.

Market impact is an assumption, not a measurement: 0.10% per side for the most-traded 500 and 0.30% for all shares, where the least-traded third is genuinely hard to trade. These rates should be varied before drawing conclusions about net returns. The paper's own cost assumption was 0.5% one way, which the run also applies to the long-short spread for comparison.

The rates were written from general knowledge of Indian charges and were not checked against a current broker's published schedule during the session.
